VALMIKI RAMAYANA - BALA KANDA - SARGA 1
========================================

Verse 1
-------
Sanskrit: तपःस्वाध्यायनिरतं तपस्वी वाग्विदां वरम् |
नारदं परिपप्रच्छ वाल्मीकिर्मुनिपुङ्गवम् || १-१-१

English: The ascetic Valmiki inquired of Narada—who was deeply engaged in austerity and study of Vedas, best among the eloquent and eminent among sages.

Verse 2
-------
Sanskrit: को न्वस्मिन् साम्प्रतं लोके गुणवान् कश्च वीर्यवान् |
धर्मज्ञश्च कृतज्ञश्च सत्यवाक्यो दृढव्रतः || १-१-२

English: "Who really is that person in this present world, who is virtuous and vigorous, conscientious, mindful of good deeds done to him, a speaker of truth and determined in his deed?"

Verse 3
-------
Sanskrit: चारित्रेण च को युक्तः सर्वभूतेषु को हितः |
विद्वान् कः कः समर्थश्च कश्चैकप्रियदर्शनः || १-१-३

English: "Who is appropriate in disposition, interested in welfare of all beings, an adept one, an able one also, and uniquely pleasant to look at?"

Verse 4
-------
Sanskrit: आत्मवान् को जितक्रोधो द्युतिमान् कोऽनसूयकः |
कस्य बिभ्यति देवाश्च जातरोषस्य संयुगे || १-१-४

English: "Who is self-composed, with controlled ire, brilliant, non-jealous, and whom do even the gods fear when provoked to war?"

Verse 5
-------
Sanskrit: एतदिच्छाम्यहं श्रोतुं परं कौतूहलं हि मे |
महर्षे त्वं समर्थोऽसि ज्ञातुमेवंविधं नरम् || १-१-५

English: "All this I wish to listen from you, O Great Sage. You are competent to know this kind of man, and indeed my inquisitiveness is immense."

Verse 6
-------
Sanskrit: श्रुत्वा चैतत्त्रिलोकज्ञो वाल्मीकेर्नारदो वचः |
श्रूयतामिति चामन्त्र्य प्रहृष्टो वाक्यमब्रवीत् || १-१-६

English: On listening to these words of Valmiki, Narada, the knower of all three worlds, said "let it be heard" and being delighted, spoke these words.

Verse 7
-------
Sanskrit: बहवो दुर्लभाश्चैव ये त्वया कीर्तिता गुणाः |
मुने वक्ष्याम्यहं बुद्ध्वा तैर्युक्तः श्रूयतां नरः || १-१-७

English: "O Sage Valmiki, the merits you have extolled are many and unattainable. I, after having known from Brahma of such a man with all these merits, will speak on. Let it be heard."

Verse 8
-------
Sanskrit: इक्ष्वाकुवंशप्रभवो रामो नाम जनैः श्रुतः |
नियतात्मा महावीर्यो द्युतिमान् धृतिमान् वशी || १-१-८

English: "One emerged from Ikshvaku dynasty, known to people as Rama by name. He is with a controlled self, highly valorous, resplendent, steadfast and a controller of his own senses."

Verse 9
-------
Sanskrit: बुद्धिमान्नीतिमान्वाग्ग्मी श्रीमान् शत्रुनिबर्हणः |
विपुलांसो महाबाहुः कम्बुग्रीवो महाहनुः || १-१-९

English: "He is wise, a moralist, eloquent, glorious, and destroyer of enemies. His arms are lengthy, with three lines in the neck indicative of good fortune, and cheekbones high."

Verse 10
--------
Sanskrit: महोरस्को महेष्वासो गूढजत्रुररिन्दमः |
आजानुबाहुः सुशिराः सुललाटः सुविक्रमः || १-१-१०

English: "He is lion-chested, with concealed collarbones, knee-length are his arms, and his is a long bow, an enemy-subjugator, and his emperor's countenance is with a crowning-head and an ample forehead, and his gait is majestic."

Verse 11
--------
Sanskrit: समः समविभक्ताङ्गः स्निग्धवर्णः प्रतापवान् |
पीनवक्षा विशालाक्षो लक्ष्मीवान् शुभलक्षणः || १-१-११

English: "He is built proportionately physically, with limbs poised symmetrically, sinew-chested, wide-eyed, complexioned charmingly. He is prosperous with all auspicious features."

Verse 12
--------
Sanskrit: धर्मज्ञः सत्यसन्धश्च प्रजानां च हिते रतः |
यशस्वी ज्ञानसंपन्नः शुचिर्वश्यः समाधिमान् || १-१-१२

English: "He is the knower of rectitude, bidden by truth, concerned in welfare of subjects, famed, proficient in prudence, clean in conduct, self-controlled and attentive."

Verse 13
--------
Sanskrit: प्रजापतिसमः श्रीमान् धाता रिपुनिषूदनः |
रक्षिता जीवलोकस्य धर्मस्य परिरक्षिता || १-१-१३

English: "He equals Lord Vishnu, the Lord of all creatures, Sustainer of the Universe, together with Sri Lakshmi, Eliminator of enemies, Protector of the world of living creatures and complete Guardian of probity."

Verse 14
--------
Sanskrit: रक्षिता स्वस्य धर्मस्य स्वजनस्य च रक्षिता |
वेदवेदाङ्गतत्त्वज्ञो धनुर्वेदे च निष्ठितः || १-१-१४

English: "He is the preserver of his own righteousness and champion of his people's welfare, a scholar in the essence of Vedas and their ancillaries, and an expert in the science of archery."

Verse 15
--------
Sanskrit: सर्वशास्त्रार्थतत्त्वज्ञः स्मृतिमान् प्रतिभानवान् |
सर्वलोकप्रियः साधुरदीनात्मा विचक्षणः || १-१-१५

English: "He is cognizant of the essence and meaning of all scriptures, excellent at memory, brilliant, dear to all worlds, gentle, level-headed and clear-headed in discriminating."

Verse 16
--------
Sanskrit: सर्वदाभिगतः सद्भिः समुद्र इव सिन्धुभिः |
आर्यः सर्वसमश्चैव सदैव प्रियदर्शनः || १-१-१६

English: "Like an ocean reached by rivers, that reverential one is always accessible by clean-minded ones, treats all equally, and is ever a feast to the eye."

Verse 17
--------
Sanskrit: स च सर्वगुणोपेतः कौसल्यानन्दवर्धनः |
समुद्र इव गाम्भीर्ये धैर्येण हिमवानिव || १-१-१७

English: "He who betters the happiness of his mother Kausalya is embodied with all noble merits, in profundity he is like an unfathomable ocean, and by fortitude unalterable like the Himalayan mountain."

Verse 18
--------
Sanskrit: विष्णुना सदृशो वीर्ये सोमवत्प्रियदर्शनः |
कालाग्निसदृशः क्रोधे क्षमया पृथिवीसमः || १-१-१८

English: "In valour comparable to Vishnu, in looks attractive like the moon, in anger matchable to world-ending fire, and in forgiveness equals the earth."

Verse 19
--------
Sanskrit: धनदेन समस्त्यागे सत्ये धर्म इवापरः |
तमेवं गुणसम्पन्नं रामं सत्यपराक्रमम् || १-१-१९

English: "In benevolence identical to Kubera God of Wealth, and in candour he is like Dharma itself, the Truth personified, with none to rival him. That Rama possessed of such merits and true valour..."

Verse 20
--------
Sanskrit: ज्येष्ठं श्रेष्ठगुणैर्युक्तं प्रियं दशरथस्सुतम् |
प्रकृतीनां हितैर्युक्तं प्रकृतिप्रियकाम्यया || १-१-२०

English: "...the eldest son, endowed with best qualities, dear to Dasharatha, concerned for welfare of the people..."

Verse 21
--------
Sanskrit: यौवराज्येन संयोक्तुमैच्छत् प्रीत्या महीपतिः |
तस्याभिषेकसंभारान् दृष्ट्वा भार्याथ कैकयी || १-१-२१

English: "...Lord of the land King Dasharatha with an intent for doing good to the people, affectionately desired to endow with regent-hood. Then seeing the arrangements for anointment, his wife Queen Kaikeyi..."

Verse 22
--------
Sanskrit: पुर्वं दत्तवरा देवी वरमेनमयाचत |
विवासनं च रामस्य भरतस्याभिषेचनम् || १-१-२२

English: "...the queen, having been previously given boons, solicited for the banishment of Rama and anointment of Bharata as boons."

Verse 23
--------
Sanskrit: स सत्यवचनाद् राजा धर्मपाशेन संयतः |
विवासयामास सुतं रामं दशरथः प्रियम् || १-१-२३

English: "Bound by his true plighted word and by the noose of righteousness, that king Dasharatha exiled his dear son Rama to forests."

Verse 24
--------
Sanskrit: स जगाम वनं वीरः प्रतिज्ञामनुपालयन् |
पितुर्वचननिर्देशात् कैकेय्याः प्रियकारणात् || १-१-२४

English: "That warrior Rama went to forest for pleasing Kaikeyi, and as directed by his father, and to follow his father's word of honour."

Verse 25
--------
Sanskrit: तं व्रजन्तं प्रियो भ्राता लक्ष्मणोऽनुजगाम ह |
स्नेहाद्विनयसम्पन्नः सुमित्रानन्दवर्धनः || १-१-२५

English: "While that Rama was going on exile, Lakshmana, endowed with humbleness, and augmentor of his mother Sumitra's joy, and a loving and dear brother to Rama, followed him out of affection..."

Verse 26
--------
Sanskrit: भ्रातरं दयितो भ्रातुः सौभ्रात्रमनुदर्शयन् |
रामस्य दयिता भार्या नित्यं प्राणसमा हिता || १-१-२६

English: "...showing brotherly fraternity. Even Seetha, Rama's beloved wife equalling to Rama's life, ever well disposed..."

Verse 27
--------
Sanskrit: जनकस्य कुले जाता देवमायेव निर्मिता |
सर्वलक्षणसम्पन्ना नारीणामुत्तमा वधूः || १-१-२७

English: "...born in the family of Janaka and one as though fashioned by Divine marvel, endowed with all good characteristics and the best among women..."

Verse 28
--------
Sanskrit: सीताप्यनुगता रामं शशिनं रोहिणी यथा |
पौरैरनुगतो दूरं पित्रा दशरथेन च || १-१-२८

English: "...followed Rama to forest, like Rohini following the Moon. Followed by citizens and by father Dasharatha for a long distance..."

Verse 29
--------
Sanskrit: शृङ्गिबेरपुरे सूतं गङ्गाकूले व्यसर्जयत् |
गुहमासाद्य धर्मात्मा निषादाधिपतिं प्रियम् || १-१-२९

English: "...virtuous Rama reached the tribal chief named Guha, who liked Rama, on the bank of River Ganga in a town called Sringaberapura..."

Verse 30
--------
Sanskrit: गुहेन सहितो रामो लक्ष्मणेन च सीतया |
ते वनेन वनं गत्वा नदीस्तीर्त्वा बहूदकाः || १-१-३०

English: "Together with Guha, Lakshmana and Seetha, he left off the charioteer. They, going from forest to forest and crossing rivers with much waters..."

Verse 31
--------
Sanskrit: चित्रकूटमनुप्राप्य भरद्वाजस्य शासनात् |
रम्यमावसथं कृत्वा रममाणा वने त्रयः || १-१-३१

English: "...by the ordainment of sage Bharadwaja, arrived at Chitrakuta, and building a handsome cottage there, they lived happily..."

Verse 32
--------
Sanskrit: देवगन्धर्वसंकाशास्तत्र ते न्यवसन् सुखम् |
चित्रकूटं गते रामे पुत्रशोकातुरस्तदा || १-१-३२

English: "...delightfully in the woods there, like gods and gandharvas. On Rama going to Chitrakuta thus, King Dasharatha tormented by grief for son..."

Verse 33
--------
Sanskrit: राजा दशरथः स्वर्गं जगाम विलपन् सुतम् |
मृते तु तस्मिन् भरतो वसिष्ठप्रमुखैर्द्विजैः || १-१-३३

English: "...went to heavens weeping for his son. On Dasharatha's dying, though Bharata had been appointed for kingship by Sage Vasishta and other Brahmanas..."

Verse 34
--------
Sanskrit: नियुज्यमानो राज्याय नैच्छद् राज्यं महाबलः |
स जगाम वनं वीरो रामपादप्रसादकः || १-१-३४

English: "...that highly mighty one did not desire kingdom. That warrior Bharata went to forest to propitiate the venerable Rama."

Verse 35
--------
Sanskrit: गत्वा तु स महात्मानं रामं सत्यपराक्रमम् |
अयाचद्भ्रातरं राममार्यभावपुरस्कृतः || १-१-३५

English: "That Bharata, possessed of worthy way of thinking, on going to Rama, begged his brother Rama, a great souled one and truly courageous one..."

Verse 36
--------
Sanskrit: त्वमेव राजा धर्मज्ञ इति रामं वचोऽब्रवीत् |
रामोऽपि परमोदारः सुमुखः सुमहायशाः || १-१-३६

English: "Bharata spoke words in this manner to Rama: 'You alone, the knower of probity, be the king.' Even though Rama was very benevolent, gracious also, highly famed..."

Verse 37
--------
Sanskrit: न चैच्छत्पितुरादेशाद्राज्यं रामो महाबलः |
पादुके चास्य राज्याय न्यासं दत्त्वा पुनः पुनः || १-१-३७

English: "...and with great prowess, Rama did not desire kingdom due to father's injunction. Thereafter, repeatedly turning Bharata back, the elder brother Rama gave his wooden shoes to Bharata..."

Verse 38
--------
Sanskrit: निवर्तयामास ततो भरतं भरताग्रजः |
स काममनवाप्यैव रामपादावुपस्पृशन् || १-१-३८

English: "...and entrusting them to represent him for the sake of kingdom. That Bharata, thus with a desire unfulfilled, ruled the kingdom from Nandigrama, touching Rama's sandals..."

Verse 39
--------
Sanskrit: नन्दिग्रामेऽकरोद्राज्यं रामागमनकाङ्क्षया |
गते तु भरते श्रीमान् सत्यसन्धो जितेन्द्रियः || १-१-३९

English: "...with an expectation of Rama's arrival. On the departure of Bharata, that splendid Rama, truth-bound, and self-controlled..."

Verse 40
--------
Sanskrit: रामस्तु पुनरालक्ष्य नागरस्य जनस्य च |
तत्रागमनमेकाग्रो दण्डकान्प्रविवेश ह || १-१-४०

English: "...anticipated the repeated visits of citizens and other people. To be undisturbed, Rama then entered Dandaka forests."

Verse 41
--------
Sanskrit: प्रविश्य तु महारण्यं रामो राजीवलोचनः |
विराधं राक्षसं हत्वा शरभङ्गं ददर्श ह || १-१-४१

English: "That lotus-eyed Rama, on his entering the vast Dandaka forest, killed the demon Viradha, and indeed beheld Sage Sharabhanga..."

Verse 42
--------
Sanskrit: सुतीक्ष्णं चाप्यगस्त्यं च अगस्त्यभ्रातरं तथा |
अगस्त्यवचनाच्चैव जग्राहैन्द्रं शरासनम् || १-१-४२

English: "...and also Sage Suteekshna, Sage Agastya and likewise Sage Agastya's brother. On Sage Agastya's word, Rama, with great delight, received from Agastya, Indra's bow..."

Verse 43
--------
Sanskrit: खड्गं च परमप्रीतस्तूणी चाक्षयसायकौ |
वसतस्तस्य रामस्य वने वनचरैः सह || १-१-४३

English: "...along with the sword and two quivers with ever replenishing arrows. During Rama's stay in the forest, all the sages together with other forest dwellers..."

Verse 44
--------
Sanskrit: ऋषयोऽभ्यागमन्सर्वे वधायासुररक्षसाम् |
स तेषां प्रतिशुश्राव राक्षसानां तथा वने || १-१-४४

English: "...approached him for the elimination of ogres and demons. He agreed to that supplication of those sages dwelling in the forest of demons..."

Verse 45
--------
Sanskrit: प्रतिज्ञातश्च रामेण वधः संयति रक्षसाम् |
ऋषीणामग्निकल्पानां दण्डकारण्यवासिनाम् || १-१-४५

English: "A promise was also made by Rama to the sages who equalled the fire and who were living in that Dandaka forest about killing of the demons in combat."

Verse 46
--------
Sanskrit: तेन तत्रैव वसता जनस्थाननिवासिनी |
विरूपिता शूर्पणखा राक्षसी कामरूपिणी || १-१-४६

English: "While Rama was living there in the Dandaka forest, a guise-changing demoness named Shurpanakha, who was a resident of Janasthana, a portion of Dandaka forest, was disfigured by him."

Verse 47
--------
Sanskrit: ततः शूर्पणखावाक्यादुद्युक्तान् सर्वराक्षसान् |
खरं त्रिशिरसं चैव दूषणं चैव राक्षसम् || १-१-४७

English: "Then in a battle, Rama killed all demons who became zealously active incited by Shurpanakha's words, including the demons Khara, Trishira, Duushana..."

Verse 48
--------
Sanskrit: निजघान रणे रामस्तेषां चैव पदानुगान् |
वने तस्मिन्निवसता जनस्थाननिवासिनाम् || १-१-४८

English: "...together with all of their foot-followers. During Rama's stay in that forest, fourteen thousand inhabitant demons of Janasthana..."

Verse 49
--------
Sanskrit: रक्षसां निहतान्यासन् सहस्राणि चतुर्दश |
ततो ज्ञातिवधं श्रुत्वा रावणः क्रोधमूर्छितः || १-१-४९

English: "...became slain. Then, on hearing the slaughter of his kinsmen, Ravana convulsed in anger..."

Verse 50
--------
Sanskrit: सहायं वरयामास मारीचं नाम राक्षसम् |
वार्यमाणः सुबहुशो मारीचेन स रावणः || १-१-५०

English: "...and sought to get the help of a demon named Maareecha. But Maareecha restrained Ravana telling him many times..."

Verse 51
--------
Sanskrit: न विरोधो बलवता क्षमो रावण तेन ते |
अनादृत्य तु तद्वाक्यं रावणः कालचोदितः || १-१-५१

English: "'Oh Ravana! Rivalry with that formidable Rama is not appropriate of you.' Then disregarding Maareecha's words and impelled by his own fate..."

Verse 52
--------
Sanskrit: जगाम सहमारीचस्तस्याश्रमपदं तदा |
तेन मायाविना दूरमपवाह्य नृपात्मजौ || १-१-५२

English: "...Ravana advanced to the place of Rama's hermitage along with Maareecha. After taking away of Rama and Lakshmana to a distance by the trickster Maareecha..."

Verse 53
--------
Sanskrit: जहार भार्यां रामस्य गृध्रं हत्वा जटायुषम् |
गृध्रं च निहतं दृष्ट्वा हृतां श्रुत्वा च मैथिलीम् || १-१-५३

English: "...Ravana stole Rama's wife after killing Jatayu. Raghava on seeing the smitten Jatayu and also on hearing about Seetha's abduction..."

Verse 54
--------
